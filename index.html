big mesh studios

A voxel editor, a rigger to move what it draws, and a world that wears it.

rm-stacker

Draw the six faces of a box and it becomes a voxel model. Save it, or publish it to your own account for anyone to use.

Open the editor

voxelscape

An endless world of generated terrain to walk through, build in, and share with whoever else is in it. Its monsters wear models drawn next door.

Enter the world

voxel-rigger

Build a skeleton, hang the parts of a voxel model on it, and set it walking. The motions are baked back into the model, ready for a world to wear.

Open the rigger

spacescape

An endless signed distance field to walk through and cut into. Paint it, script it, and save places that anybody can open.

Enter the landscape

sdf-modeller

Build a figure out of placed primitives. Union them, subtract from them, colour them, and move them with the handles.

Open the modeller

They are open source, in one repository: github.com/big-mesh-studios/big-mesh-studios.
