# Model assets

The models under this directory are voxel figures in the rm-stacker format: a
box a part, six indexed images a face, and a shared palette. Most were drawn in
`rm-stacker` for this world and are original work. One is generated from a
third-party rig, and its source is recorded here.

- `player-human.zip` — the human avatar, ten boxes bound to a CC0 skeleton, with
  its `idle`, `walk` and `run` motions baked onto them. Generated by
  `apps/voxel-rigger`'s `pnpm make:human` from two assets of that app:
  `public/animations/human.glb` and `public/models/human.zip`. The rig is
  `Barbarian.glb` from KayKit Character Pack — Adventurers
  (https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0)
  by Kay Lousberg (https://www.kaylousberg.com), trimmed to its joint tree and
  four clips; the boxes are `voxel-rigger`'s own, one per bone, drawn where that
  bone rests. Creative Commons Zero requires no attribution; this note records
  provenance only, and `apps/voxel-rigger/ATTRIBUTION.md` records the same
  chain with the trimming script beside it.
