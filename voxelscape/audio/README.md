# Audio assets

- `rain.ogg` — "Soft rain" field recording (61s), Creative Commons Zero.
  Source: Freesound #640655 by barkenov
  (https://freesound.org/people/barkenov/sounds/640655/), re-encoded to Ogg
  Vorbis. CC0 requires no attribution; this note records provenance only.
- `thunder.ogg` — an 11s close thunder clap and rolling tail cut from a
  longer CC0 field recording of distant rolling thunder. Source: Freesound
  #717890 by TRP
  (https://freesound.org/people/TRP/sounds/717890/), re-encoded and
  loudness-normalized to Ogg Vorbis. CC0 requires no attribution; this note
  records provenance only.
- `letter-tick.ogg` — a 10 ms interface "tick", the per-letter typewriter sound
  for NPC dialog. Source: Kenney's Interface Sounds pack, `click_002.ogg`
  (https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), Creative Commons Zero, kept as
  the Ogg Vorbis the pack ships. CC0 requires no attribution; this note records
  provenance only.

The Zombies: The Mansion game sounds, all Creative Commons Zero and re-encoded
to mono Ogg Vorbis (44.1 kHz, ~32 kbps) with loudness normalized to
I=-16, TP=-1.5 dB LUFS, the same target the recordings above use:
- `zombie-growl.ogg` — a 0.34s zombie snarl, played when a zombie swings at a
  player. Source: ianzazz's "Zombie noises and moans" pack,
  `fastzombie1.ogg` (https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-noises-and-moans).
- `zombie-die.ogg` — a 0.67s zombie wail, played when a zombie dies. Source:
  same pack, `zombienoise3.ogg`.
- `wave-eerie.ogg` — a 2.1s mournful moan that hangs in the air just before a
  new wave pours in. Source: same pack, `zombienoise1.ogg`.
- `wave-complete.ogg` — a 0.47s rising chime that closes a cleared wave.
  Source: Kenney's Digital Audio pack, `powerUp2.ogg`
  (https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio).
- `gun-pistol.ogg` — the Starter Pistol's shot (`laserSmall_000.ogg`).
- `gun-machine.ogg` — the Horde Grinder's shot (`laserSmall_001.ogg`).
- `gun-rifle.ogg` — the Vault Rifle's shot (`laserRetro_000.ogg`).
- `gun-shotgun.ogg` — the Room Sweeper's boom (`explosionCrunch_000.ogg`).
  All four from Kenney's Sci-fi Sounds pack
  (https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds).
